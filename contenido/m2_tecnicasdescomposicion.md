## Two Main Approaches
- Top-down approach.
- Modularization.

### Top-Down Approach
A method of problem-solving that begins with a broad overview of a problem and breaks it down into smaller, more manageable parts.

#### Top-Down Approach Methodology
- Similar to creating an outline.
- Aligns with deductive reasoning.
  * Start with a general statement of the problem
  * Discover the reasons that are the cause of the problem.

#### Top-Down Approach Example
1. Define the goal: Build an e-commerce site.
2. Decompose the development tasks into sections.
  - Homepage
  - Product pages
  - Checkout process
3. Decompose the sections into smaller tasks.
  - Design the loyout
  - Code the functionality
  - Test the features

### Modularization
A decomposition approach where you fragment a complex issue into smaller, independent parts that each carry out a specific role.
- Break the problem into units or modules.
- Modules perform a specific function.
- Simplifies management and understanding.
